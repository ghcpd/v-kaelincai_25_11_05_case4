- Project B (Deterministic): Each scorer writes into an idempotent per-question map keyed on scorer id. The aggregator reduces deterministically (average of unique scorer results). Late or retried responses update the entry for that scorer rather than being double-added; reduction is order-insensitive so aggregate is deterministic.

- Root causes (Project A):

Limitations
- The deterministic approach assumes scorers are idempotent or can be uniquely identified; if external scorers are truly non-idempotent with different stateful effects, more advanced coordination needed (versioned results, consensus, or transaction-like commit).
